Privacy Policy for Skolefinder

Effective date: 15 July 2026

Skolefinder is an app for finding and comparing Danish primary schools. We built it with respect for your privacy: you need no account, and we do not track you across apps or websites.

This English text is a translation of the authoritative Danish version. In case of any discrepancy, the Danish text prevails.

Data controller

Skolefinder
Contact: shimj9312@gmail.com

What data we process

Crash and error reports

If the app crashes or hits an error, it automatically sends a technical report to Sentry (our error-monitoring tool). A report contains technical information such as:

  • the error's stack trace (where in the code the error occurred),
  • device model, operating system and OS version,
  • app version,
  • an anonymous session status (whether the app session ended normally or with a crash).

These reports are used solely to find and fix bugs. We do not use performance tracing, session replay or similar features. Sentry is hosted in the EU (Germany), and reports are deleted automatically after about 90 days (Sentry's default retention).

The legal basis is our legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)) in keeping the app stable and bug-free.

Location

If you grant permission, the app uses your location to find your municipality (kommune) so it can show nearby schools. Your coordinates are sent to our server, which looks up the municipality via Dataforsyningen's (DAWA) address service. The coordinates are not stored in any user profile or database. They may appear briefly in the server's operational logs and — if a lookup fails — in a technical error report. You can decline location access at any time in your phone's settings; the app still works but will not detect your municipality automatically.

Server logs

Our server temporarily keeps standard web-request logs (IP address, app version, device model, operating system and the requested address) for operations and security.

What we do NOT do

  • No user account and no login.
  • No advertising.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs (e.g. Google Analytics, Facebook, etc.).
  • No selling or sharing of your data with third parties for marketing.

Your favorites and settings (such as language and theme) are stored only locally on your device.

In-app purchases

You can support Skolefinder with a single, optional purchase. The purchase is handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details — the app only knows whether your store account has made the purchase.

School data

All school data comes from STIL (Styrelsen for It og Læring) via Uddannelsesstatistik.dk. Skolefinder is not affiliated with or endorsed by STIL.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct or delete information about you, and to object to our processing. Because we have no user accounts, the only data that could be linked to you is the temporary error and operational data described above. Contact us at shimj9312@gmail.com to exercise your rights or ask questions. You may also lodge a complaint with the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet, datatilsynet.dk).

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and the version available in the app and online.
