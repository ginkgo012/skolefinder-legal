Privacy Policy for Skolefinder

Effective date: 6 September 2026

Skolefinder is an app for finding and comparing Danish primary schools. We built it with respect for your privacy: you need no account, and we do not track you across apps or websites.

This English text is a translation of the authoritative Danish version. In case of any discrepancy, the Danish text prevails.

Data controller

Skolefinder
Contact: info@skolefinder.dk

What data we process

Install id and support code

The first time you open the app, it creates a random install id. It is generated on your device, is not derived from your phone's id (neither the advertising id nor the manufacturer's device id), and is stored locally on the device.

The id is sent along every time the app fetches data from our own server, and it is included in error reports to Sentry. It has one purpose: so that a message about a problem can be connected to the requests and errors it is about. The app has no accounts, so without the id there is nothing to connect to, and we cannot tell which lines in the logs belong to your message.

The first eight characters make up your support code. If you write to us from the app, the code is attached to the message automatically, where you can see it and delete it before sending.

About the id:

  • it is not shared with other apps or companies,
  • it is not used for advertising and is never combined with data from other companies,
  • it says nothing about who you are — we know neither name nor email,
  • it disappears when you delete the app; a new install gets a new id.

The id is stable until you delete the app. We therefore treat the error and operational data it appears in as personal data, even though we cannot see who you are. The legal basis is our legitimate interest (GDPR art. 6(1)(f)) in being able to troubleshoot specific reports.

Crash and error reports

If the app crashes or hits an error, it automatically sends a technical report to Sentry (our error-monitoring tool). A report contains technical information such as:

  • the error's stack trace (where in the code the error occurred),
  • device model, operating system and OS version,
  • app version,
  • your install id (described above),
  • an anonymous session status (whether the app session ended normally or with a crash).

These reports are used solely to find and fix bugs. We do not use performance tracing, session replay or similar features. Sentry is hosted in the EU (Germany), and reports are deleted automatically after 30 days (the retention period on our Sentry plan).

The legal basis is our legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)) in keeping the app stable and bug-free.

Location

If you grant permission, the app uses your location to find your municipality (kommune) so it can show nearby schools. Your coordinates are sent to our server, which looks up the municipality via Dataforsyningen's (DAWA) address service. The coordinates are not stored in any user profile or database. They may appear briefly in the server's operational logs and — if a lookup fails — in a technical error report. You can decline location access at any time in your phone's settings; the app still works but will not detect your municipality automatically.

Server logs

Our server temporarily keeps standard web-request logs (IP address, app version, device model, operating system, your install id, whether Skolefinder Plus has been bought, the requested address and — for school comparisons — how many schools the comparison held) for operations and security, and — in counted, aggregate form — to gauge how much the app's features are used and what share of installs have bought Plus. The logs are kept for about seven days and then deleted automatically; only the aggregate counts are retained.

What we do NOT do

  • No user account and no login.
  • No advertising.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs (e.g. Google Analytics, Facebook, etc.).
  • No selling or sharing of your data with third parties for marketing.

Your favorites and settings (such as language and theme) are stored only locally on your device.

In-app purchases

Skolefinder Plus is a single purchase that supports the app and unlocks every paid feature — those that exist now and any that come later. Today that is sending the printout as a PDF file. Every figure, school year and chart is free for everyone and stays that way. The purchase is handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details — the app only knows whether your store account has made the purchase, and remembers that on the device so your paid features still work when the store cannot be reached. If you have bought Plus, the app sends a Plus flag when it fetches data from our server; it is used only to count what share of installs have bought (see Server logs).

School data

All school data comes from STIL (Styrelsen for It og Læring) via Uddannelsesstatistik.dk. Skolefinder is not affiliated with or endorsed by STIL.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct or delete information about you, and to object to our processing. Because we have no user accounts, the only data that could be linked to you is the error and operational data described above, which carries your install id. If you give us your support code we can find it; without it we cannot, because we have no other way to recognise you. Contact us at info@skolefinder.dk to exercise your rights or ask questions. You may also lodge a complaint with the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet, datatilsynet.dk).

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and the version available in the app and online.
